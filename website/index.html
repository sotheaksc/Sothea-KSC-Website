Our craft

Three techniques, all by hand

Every piece starts as plain silver. The pattern comes from a hammer, a chisel and many hours of patient work.

Engraving

Leaves and flowers in the Khmer kbach style are cut into the band of each anklet, line by line.

Repoussé

The silver is hammered from the inside to raise the design, then chased from the front to sharpen every petal.

Filigree

Fine silver wire is twisted and soldered into open beads and rosettes, then strung into necklaces and bracelets.
